# WordPress websites built for creators and what comes next.

Made to Thrive is an all-female web agency helping content brands and food creators turn their websites into lasting business assets.

With **more than a decade of experience** and **over 250 custom website launches**, we combine strategy, thoughtful design, and WordPress expertise to build sites that support audience growth, simplify publishing, and create opportunities for revenue.

## Built around the way creators work

From high-traffic recipe libraries to affiliate-driven content brands, we build for the details that matter: intuitive editing, content discovery, monetization, accessibility, and performance.

## The code behind our work

We develop WordPress themes, plugins, and tools to solve real publishing needs across our client websites. Our approach centers on WordPress core, the block editor, and reusable solutions that remain easy to maintain.

- **WordPress-native editing.** Block themes, patterns, and editor controls that give creators control over their content.
- **Reusable functionality.** Focused plugins and blocks that support everyday publishing workflows.
- **Accessibility and performance.** Semantic markup, keyboard-friendly interfaces, responsive layouts, and carefully scoped assets.
- **Maintainable foundations.** PHP, HTML, CSS/SCSS, and vanilla JavaScript, with clear ownership between content, themes, and plugins.

## A partnership that continues beyond launch

We stay involved from the first strategy conversation through launch and ongoing improvements.

1. **Strategy & design** — clarify goals, plan content and user experience, and create a design that feels like your brand.
2. **Development & launch** — build and refine your custom WordPress website, support migrations, and help you feel confident managing it.
3. **Optimization & support** — review performance, SEO, accessibility, and site health, then keep improving as your business evolves.
